# GPT-Mecha 40 — Concept brief

## Design language: slim knight

The selected direction is **C — slim knight proportions**:
- slim waist and narrow shoulders
- long-leg visual proportion
- sharp, layered armor surfaces
- agile rather than tank-like silhouette
- white/silver primary armor, navy secondary armor and cyan light accents
- original helmet and faceplate with expressive cyan eyes
- compact chest core and removable rear expansion pack

## Proportion target

The initial target is a 40–42 cm class robot. Exact dimensions are pending the reference skeleton CAD audit.

Visual proportion is not permission to alter structural link lengths. The armor may create a longer-leg appearance, but it must not change actuator axis locations or interfere with the walking mechanism.

## Functional appearance

- Head: camera aperture integrated into forehead/visor, eye display, microphone openings and two-axis head motion.
- Chest: removable front armor, status/core light, ventilation and access to compute modules.
- Waist: narrow cosmetic shell around the structural pelvis; service access on rear/side.
- Legs: segmented thigh, knee and shin armor, all outside joint swept volumes.
- Feet: armor must not reduce the reference support polygon or hide contact switches.
- Backpack: decorative and modular only in V1; no propellers or flight hardware.

## Design constraint

This is an original design inspired by a general light-knight mecha aesthetic. Do not copy a named character's exact head, silhouette, ornament, emblem or proprietary model.
